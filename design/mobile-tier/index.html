JulineMart mobile tier: wave 1 previews

Static phone mockups (390 × 844, Android) of the redesigned product page, category listing and search, cart and checkout. They follow Material 3 structure (top app bars, a navigation bar with an active indicator, chips, bottom sheets, snackbars), themed with JulineMart purple and orange. The homepage is not included: it keeps its current design. Each phone scrolls on its own.

Real data: product names, photos, prices, ratings and sellers from the live catalogue Illustrative: delivery dates, delivery fees, the customer name and address

Product page

Edge-to-edge photo with the actions floating over it. The buy bar stays pinned, so Add to cart never scrolls away. Colour is picked inline, and delivery, protection and returns are one tap away.

Category listing and search

A compact top bar with the category name, then a row of chips for sort and filters. The grid is two even columns with one card shape everywhere. Filters open in a bottom sheet, and Back closes it.

Cart and checkout

The cart groups items by seller, because each seller ships separately. Checkout is one calm page of cards with a pinned "Pay" button showing the exact total. The navigation bar is hidden during checkout so nothing pulls the shopper away.

Design system used here: Material 3 colour roles from brand purple #77088a and orange #ff7a29; Inter on the Material type scale (body 16/24, title 22/28, label 14/20); 16px screen margin on a 4px grid; touch targets of at least 48px; 12px card and 28px sheet corners; standard easing cubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1).
