Homepage on phones: preview

The homepage keeps its look and its order: hero, categories, gifts, product rows, stores, reviews. What changes is that it joins the rest of the phone app: the same top bar and bottom tab bar as every other page, the same product cards, calmer section headings, and a short help area instead of the desktop footer. Desktop is not changing. Each phone scrolls on its own.

What you decide

  1. Option A or B for the hero and the product rows. Recommended: A (closest to today, lighter on mobile data), optionally with B's peeking hero.
  2. Side-panel ads: each desktop side panel takes several ads and slides; on phones all of them play in one small carousel in the middle of the homepage. Phones show the same tall images you already upload, scaled down, so nothing extra is needed. At this size the strip is about 180px tall and the headlines stay readable. "Get the app" is hidden inside the Android app.
  3. Campaigns: a row right under the hero while a campaign is live, and the pop-up turns into a bottom sheet on phones (desktop unchanged).
  4. Category colours: today's five colours (A) or one purple family (B).
  5. Flash sale countdown and discount tags: your products have no sale end time or "was" price yet, so these only appear once that data is added in admin.
  6. Broken flash-sale photos: 8 of the 12 flash-sale products still load images from the old WordPress site, so they show blank today. They need new photos in admin either way.
